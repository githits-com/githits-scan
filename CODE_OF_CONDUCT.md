# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in this project's issues, pull requests, discussions,
and other community spaces is expected to treat others respectfully.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence; criticize the work without attacking people.
- Welcome newcomers, explain technical disagreements, and accept constructive feedback.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and accessibility needs.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish someone's personal information or private
  messages without permission.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
and repeated disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to [support@githits.com](mailto:support@githits.com),
the [GitHits support contact](https://githits.com/support/), with links or other
context where safe to share. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue.
For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers review reports, consider context, and protect the reporter's privacy
when handling them. Depending on the conduct and its impact, maintainers may ask
for a correction, remove content, limit participation, or exclude a participant.
Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is itself a violation.
