# Open backlog

## Authoritative reasons for joint remediation changes

Joint change lists and retained dependency requests explain current relationships,
but do not prove why a particular parent change was selected. The service owns
that causal reasoning; its current consumed change contract has no typed reason.
Scan therefore shows factual request context where available and never invents a
causal explanation from names, ranges, change order or opaque context.

Adopt authoritative per-change reasons after a compatible typed service contract
is available. Preserve old reports without reasons and unknown future vocabulary;
only explain the supplied reason and its exact identities/evidence. Acceptance:
native parent-enabling and independent/co-remediation changes have distinct,
truthful human explanations; aliases, providers, occurrences and partial scopes
remain separate; compact JSON/saved analyses follow an explicitly reviewed
additive contract, and missing reasons leave existing output usable.

This service-dependent adoption is outside the current human-presentation fix.
